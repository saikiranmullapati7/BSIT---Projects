Finland (officially the Republic of Finland, or Suomi in Finnish) is a Nordic country located in Northern Europe. It borders Sweden to the northwest, Norway to the north, Russia to the east, and faces Estonia across the Gulf of Finland to the south.

Finland is globally famous for blue and white, which are the colors of the Finnish national flag. These colors beautifully represent the country's natural landscape: the blue symbolizes Finland's thousands of lakes and the clear skies, while the white stands for the pristine snow covering the land in winter.

Finland is famously called the "land of a thousand lakes," boasting 187,888 recorded lakes within its borders. It is heavily forested, with roughly 75% of the land covered by dense boreal forests. The northernmost region is Lapland, an Arctic wilderness known for its ski resorts, the indigenous Sámi people, and the Northern Lights.
